My time as a developer

In 2023, I started my studies at SintLucas in Eindhoven, where I was introduced to development for the first time.

At first, I was introduced to basic HTML and CSS, and I used them to create my very first "website."

Later, I started learning about JS and Vue.js, and I designed the websites myself using Figma.

I completed my first internship during my 3rd year at Influid, where I learned a lot about Bootstrap, SCSS, and jQuery, as well as a bit about WordPress.

This is currently my best-known working method. I am currently in my 4th year of study and I'm working on various projects using Bootstrap, SCSS, and jQuery as well as Vue.js.
